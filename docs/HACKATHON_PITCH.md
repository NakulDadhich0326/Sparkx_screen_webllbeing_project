## 3-Minute Pitch Script for Judges

### 1. The Hook (0:00 - 0:40) — Nakul Dadhich
> *"Respected judges, every single college student in this hall has experienced this: you sit down to study for 30 minutes, you open your phone to verify a formula, and before you know it, you’ve spent 90 minutes doomscrolling Instagram Reels or YouTube Shorts.*
>
> *Existing screen time blockers fail for two critical reasons: they are trivially easy to bypass with one click, or they feel punitive and humiliating. At Team SPARKX from Government Engineering College Ajmer, we built a non-punitive, AI-assisted Digital Wellbeing Coach that turns mindless scrolling into rewarding study consistency."*

### 2. Our Uniqueness & Solution (0:40 - 1:40) — Ankit Kumar Meena & Gaurav Kumar
> *"Our solution is built on three core pillars:*
> 1. **Focus Reward:** We gamify consistency. Complete 30+ minutes of focused work, and you advance your daily streak and earn Focus Coins redeemable for study rewards.
> 2. **Live Distraction Catch:** Our mobile app utilizes front-camera vision AI to detect gaze shifts and phone-in-hand distractions. And if camera access is denied, our System Failure Fallback automatically engages a 10-second grace tab guard.
> 3. **The 3-Level Intervention Pipeline:** Rather than abruptly locking the device, we apply behavioral friction:
>    - **Level 1:** A gentle reminder toast re-anchoring student intent.
>    - **Level 2:** A 10-second 'Dim + Breathe' mindfulness pause.
>    - **Level 3:** A 30-second delay requiring the student to type why they want to exit.
>    If a session is broken, it is **logged, never shamed**!"*

### 3. Privacy, Architecture & Live Demo (1:40 - 2:40) — Bhumika Rawat & Nakul Dadhich
> *"On the backend, our Node.js and Python architectures provide real-time session synchronization, JWT authentication, and study group presence. In our peer accountability groups, friends can cheer and send virtual coffee to each other.
>
> Most importantly, privacy is paramount: only your Name and Rank appear on the leaderboard — zero browsing logs or private phone details are ever leaked.
>
> Let us show you our live mobile app in action..."*

## Demo Checklist for Judges
1. **Show Focus Ring:** Start a 35-minute session (demonstrates the 30+ min streak rule).
2. **Demonstrate Live Camera AI:** Turn on front camera preview, show gaze detection.
3. **Trigger Level 1 Toast:** Click "Simulate Reel" button; observe the gentle chime and toast.
4. **Trigger Level 2 Dim + Breathe:** Click "Simulate Reel" again; experience the 10-second animated breathing circle.
5. **Trigger Level 3 Wait + Reason:** Observe the 30-second delay lock and the non-punitive "Logged, Never Shamed" message.
6. **Show Daily Planner & Study Circles:** Open Tasks tab, show one-click focus transition, and send peer cheers.
7. **Show Privacy Leaderboard:** Point out that only student Name & Rank are visible.
